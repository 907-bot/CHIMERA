## Phase Breakdown: v2.0 to v10.0

### Phase 10: Continuum & Field Dynamics Engine (`CHIMERA v2.0`)

* **Objective**: Transition from discrete $N$-body particle dynamics to continuous partial differential equations (PDEs) representing fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, and field physics.
* **Key Components**:
  * **JAX / GPU Vectorized PDE Solver**: Navier-Stokes equations ($\frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla)\mathbf{u} = -\frac{1}{\rho}\nabla p + \nu \nabla^2 \mathbf{u}$), heat transport, and Maxwell field equations.
  * **Continuous Observatory**: Grid-based tensor telemetry sampling velocity fields, pressure gradients, and vorticity distributions.
  * **Field Symbolic Discovery**: Extension of PySINDy for spatial-temporal PDE identification ($\partial_t u = f(u, \nabla u, \nabla^2 u)$).
* **AI Scientist Role**: Identifies turbulence regimes, phase boundaries, and conservation laws in continuous media.
* **Exit Criteria**: Autonomous recovery of Navier-Stokes and heat equation parameters from field scalar grids with $< 0.5\%$ error.
